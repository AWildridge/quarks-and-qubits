Bumblebee adapts masked representation learning to particle physics. Particle four-vectors replace
word tokens, and removing positional encodings avoids assigning meaning to an arbitrary ordering
of the particles. The model uses both generator- and reconstruction-level information.

The published study evaluates dileptonic top-quark reconstruction, toponium discrimination, and
initial-state classification. See the linked paper for the training setup, benchmarks, and reported
performance. The model-card demo on this website uses synthetic profiles and is a separate teaching
tool; it is not a demonstration of trained Bumblebee weights.
