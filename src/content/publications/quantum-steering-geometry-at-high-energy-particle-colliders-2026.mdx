Quantum steering ellipsoids give a geometric picture of the conditional spin states accessible
in a pair of spin-1/2 particles. We develop collider observables from this geometry and use
top-quark pair production to study polarization, spin correlations, entanglement, and
nonlocality. The framework also relates changes in ellipsoid geometry to dimension-six
interactions, connecting quantum information measurements with searches for new physics.
