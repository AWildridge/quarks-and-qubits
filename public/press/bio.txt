AJ Wildridge - Short Bio

AJ Wildridge is a postdoctoral researcher at Fermilab in experimental high-energy particle physics. His work spans top-quark measurements, quantum-information observables, and machine-learning applications for collider data.

His current research includes spin-correlation and entanglement measurements in top-quark pair production, quantum-inspired reconstruction methods, and machine-learning tools for particle-physics discovery. He also works on the CMS Endcap Timing Layer (ETL). Wildridge regularly presents this work at workshops, conferences, and collaboration forums.

AJ Wildridge - Extended Bio

AJ Wildridge is a postdoctoral researcher at Fermilab working at the intersection of collider physics, quantum information, and machine learning. His research focuses on measurable quantum observables in high-energy particle collisions, especially top-quark spin correlations and entanglement signatures at the LHC.

He also works on the CMS Endcap Timing Layer (ETL), part of the timing detector upgrade for the High-Luminosity LHC, and develops computational methods for particle-physics reconstruction and discovery. His publications and presentations cover CMS measurements, quantum-information interpretations of collider observables, and machine-learning challenges for anomaly detection in science.
